About OLANES GLOBAL EXPORT LTD

OLANES GLOBAL EXPORT LTD is a Nigerian export company focused on sourcing and supplying quality agricultural commodities, charcoal, and natural products for local and international markets.


We aim to build reliable relationships with international buyers, wholesalers, distributors, and business partners while providing quality-focused products and professional export services.


Our approach is centered on clear communication, reliable sourcing, careful product handling, and professional coordination throughout the export process.
